How this was searched

  • Kiwi.com's public MCP server (https://mcp.kiwi.com, tool search-flight) was called over HTTP. It needs no key.
  • Destination was set to the city "Istanbul", which covers both IST and SAW. Departures 17 Nov 2026 to 30 Apr 2027, 7-8 nights, max_sector_stopovers=0, MYR.
  • Searches: all airlines with 0 and 1 checked bags, all airlines excluding Turkish Airlines, AirAsia X only with 0 and 1 bags, and Turkish Airlines only for Nov to Dec.
  • Kiwi does not price bags separately. Bag costs are the difference between the same itinerary searched with 0 and 1 checked bags.
  • The date picker comes from 24 weekly searches (non-stop only, 1 checked bag, 7-8 nights), keeping the cheapest return for each departure day. Kiwi returns 15 results per search, so days without a fare in the cheapest 15 of their week show a dash.
  • Only 15 results come back per search, so some dates may be missing. Prices were not cross-checked against Google Flights.
  • Fares change often. Reconfirm the price and bag allowance on the booking page before paying.
